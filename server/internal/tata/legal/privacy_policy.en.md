**Privacy Policy**

**Introduction**

Welcome to TATA. We value your personal information and privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, delete and protect your personal information when you use the TATA App and related services, and how you may exercise your rights.

This Policy applies to TATA services in Mainland China. Users in Taiwan and other overseas regions are subject to the privacy policy applicable to their respective regions. Where separate consent is required by law, we will provide a separate notice and obtain your consent before the relevant feature is used.

Please read this Policy carefully before using the services. We will not deny basic functions solely because you refuse to provide non-essential personal information. If certain information is necessary for a specific function, we will explain its purpose and the possible impact of not providing it.

**2. Personal Information We Collect and Purposes**

We process personal information lawfully, fairly, in good faith and only to the extent necessary to provide specific service functions. The categories and purposes are as follows.

**2.1 Account and Login Information**

When you sign in using email, WeChat or an Apple Account, we process your email address or the relevant account identifier, depending on the login method you select, for account identification and authentication. WeChat and Apple sign-in do not require you to separately bind an email address. The current version of TATA does not offer phone-number login.

**2.2 User Profile**

You may voluntarily provide a nickname, avatar, gender, date of birth and exploration preferences. Your nickname and avatar are used to display your profile; other information is used to complete your profile and provide related functions. These fields are optional, and not providing them will not affect basic sign-in or use.

**2.3 Device Information**

We collect a device ID to identify a device, determine eligibility for the new-user onboarding experience and prevent the same device from claiming new-user benefits more than once. The device ID is retained only for the period necessary for these purposes and will be deleted in accordance with applicable rules once the purpose has been achieved or the account is closed.

**2.4 Tarot and AI Interaction Content**

To provide tarot readings, AI tarot-reader conversations, fortune analysis and history services, we process the questions you submit, the spreads you select, card-draw results, AI-generated content and conversation records.

To generate AI readings, we transmit relevant information to DeepSeek for processing. When you follow up with the AI Tarot Reader, the system also uses all tarot-reading and conversation records saved under your account as context for generating the response.

**2.5 Transaction Information**

When you purchase a membership, credit top-up package, card deck or other paid service, we process the order number, product name, transaction amount, payment status and payment time to complete the transaction, deliver benefits, verify orders and handle payment issues. Payment-account details and payment credentials are handled by the relevant payment service provider under its own rules.

**2.6 Usage Records, Cookies and Marketing**

TATA currently does not retain general feature-usage records, use cookies, advertising identifiers, campaign attribution or personalized tracking, or send marketing emails, text messages or push notifications. Reminder functions are triggered locally on your device, and reminder times and settings are stored on your device.

**3. AI Models and Third-Party Processing**

3.1 TATA uses DeepSeek models to provide AI-generated content for standard readings, exploration spreads, the AI Tarot Reader, annual forecasts and daily/tomorrow forecasts. The related AI data processing takes place in Mainland China.

3.2 To generate AI results, we send the necessary questions, spreads, card-draw results, conversation content or historical context described in Section 2 to DeepSeek through an interface. Information you enter may contain personal information. Please do not submit identity-document numbers, financial-account details, detailed home addresses or sensitive information unrelated to the service.

3.3 Under TATA’s current service arrangements, DeepSeek does not retain user inputs or generated responses sent by TATA and does not use such content for model training, evaluation or service improvement. If the service arrangements or data-processing practices change, we will update this Policy and seek renewed consent where required.

3.4 TATA staff cannot view your tarot questions, AI conversations or generated content. We restrict access to relevant data through access-control measures.

3.5 TATA uses Alibaba Cloud for backend cloud services, and the database is deployed independently by TATA. The official WeChat SDK is used for WeChat login, Sign in with Apple for Apple login, and Apple App Store in-app purchases and Alipay for payments. Each provider processes necessary information within the scope of its services.

**4. Third-Party Services and Provision of Information**

To provide AI readings, account login, data storage and payment functions, we use the following third-party services to the extent necessary:

\(1\) AI service: DeepSeek, which processes tarot questions, card-draw information and AI conversations and generates relevant content.

\(2\) Cloud service: Alibaba Cloud, which provides backend servers and data-storage support; the database is deployed independently by TATA.

\(3\) Third-party login: WeChat and Apple, used for account login and identity verification when you choose the relevant method.

\(4\) Payment services: Apple App Store in-app purchases and Alipay, used to process payments, subscriptions and transaction verification.

We process personal information only to the extent necessary to provide the relevant functions. Where personal information is provided to another personal information handler, entrusted for processing, or otherwise requires separate consent by law, we will provide the required notice and fulfill applicable management and consent obligations. We do not use your tarot questions, AI conversations or avatar for advertising, public case studies, model training or product optimization.

**5. Retention and Deletion**

5.1 Account profile information, tarot history and AI conversation records are retained while your account remains active. If you delete the relevant records or close your account, we will delete the corresponding data in accordance with applicable law and product functions.

5.2 Your current avatar is stored on the server. When you replace your avatar or close your account, the previous avatar will be deleted from the server.

5.3 Orders and payment records are retained for the periods required by applicable laws and regulations. Records that must legally be retained will not be deleted early due to account closure; after the retention period expires, we will delete or anonymize them as required by law.

5.4 The device ID is used to determine eligibility for new-user benefits and prevent repeated claims. Once the purpose has been achieved or your account is closed, we will delete the relevant device ID unless otherwise required by law.

5.5 TATA does not separately create or retain long-term AI memory, conversation summaries or user profiles. To maintain continuity in AI Tarot Reader conversations, the system retrieves the tarot history and conversation records saved under your account as context when generating a response.

5.6 Data that must be retained by law will be deleted or anonymized after the statutory retention period expires. Backup data is retained under the applicable backup-management mechanism and will be deleted or anonymized when its retention period is reached.

**6. Storage Location and Cross-Border Processing**

6.1 This Policy applies to TATA services in Mainland China. The AI model used by TATA processes relevant requests in Mainland China. The specific storage locations of other data depend on the actual deployment of servers and databases.

6.2 Based on current confirmation, personnel and service providers outside Mainland China do not access Mainland China users’ tarot questions or AI conversation content. If we provide Mainland China users’ personal information overseas in the future, we will assess the applicable requirements and complete the required notices, consent and cross-border data compliance procedures.

6.3 This Policy applies only to users in Mainland China. The processing and cross-border transfer of data of users in other regions are governed by the privacy policy for the relevant region.

6.4 If the scope of this Policy or our data-processing arrangements change, we will update this Policy and provide required notices and obtain consent as necessary.
